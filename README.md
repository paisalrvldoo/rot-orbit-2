[Play ROT ORBIT on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/75703852342218)

Buy a brainrot from the orbit rail, carry it back to your bay, and dock it to earn credits. Collect at the terminal and come back for rarer cargo. Seal your bay to keep thieves and rift gremlins out. If one gets away with your cargo, chase it and zap it to send your brainrot home.

Use **E** or the on-screen prompts to interact. Press **Q** or tap **Zap** to defend your cargo. The **Logbook** tracks 42 brainrots across six rarities, plus Ion, Gilded, and Eclipse mutations. It also holds income upgrades, rebirths, and rotating contracts.

Build the place with `rojo build default.project.json -o game.rbxl`. In a Studio playtest, use **1** for the cover camera, **2** for the index, **3** for the rift camera, **4** for upgrades, and **0** to return to play. Run `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` to stage the submission and generate the dossier.
